MindShift

Meaning Change in Mental Health Terminology and Its Impact on Human–AI Interaction

MindShift aims at studying how psychiatric terms are used and interpreted across clinical and everyday contexts, how these interpretations vary between people, and whether language models can recover the intended meaning and affective significance of those uses.
